We are **USTPeeps**, a multidisciplinary team from the _University of Science and Technology of Southern Philippines – Cagayan de Oro (USTP-CDO)_. Bringing together individuals from the fields of **Data Science, Computer Science, and Computer Engineering**, we combine our skills in data analytics, technology, and innovation to build _BlueShift_ and explore how data can help address real-world environmental and socioeconomic challenges.
